FAQ

Why isn’t Helix a leaderboard of quick tricks?

We score explanations that rule out tempting but wrong patterns, testing AI’s depth.

Why explanations > patterns?

Patterns can mislead; explanations reveal causes. Helix problems (e.g., pattern-trap, clone-trap) demand step-by-step reasoning to catch errors shallow heuristics miss.

Are LLMs disqualified?

No; we amplify them with prompts demanding explanation and counterexamples.

Evaluation Rubric

From eval_protocol.md:

  • Pattern-Match Trap Rate: Fraction of items where shallow heuristics fail but explanations succeed.
  • Clone-Trap Robustness: Credit for solutions leveraging diversity over uniformity.
